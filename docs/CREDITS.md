# Credits

The recorded sounds in the resource pack come from CC0 sources. CC0 asks for no credit, but the authors are named here. Only CC0
and public domain sources are used: no CC-BY or CC-BY-SA recording is in the pack.

The other 31 sounds are made by the build script, `CoreFactory-ResourcePack/tools/sounds/make_sounds.py`, and carry no credit.
